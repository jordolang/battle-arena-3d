Shared keyboard

Controls

Select a key and press a new one to rebind it. Up to four people can share one keyboard.

Punch, punch, punch three-hit combo ending in a hook
Punch, kick jab into a front kick
Kick, kick roundhouse that knocks down
Jump, then kick diving kick
Hold block stops hits from the front; too many and your guard breaks
Special costs half the blue mana bar, which fills as you fight
Skills 1, 2 and 3 every fighter has three of their own, matched to their class: Warriors strike up close, Ranged fighters shoot, Mages cast spells from afar. Each costs mana and then cools down
Default keys for P1 move with W A S D, punch J, kick K, special I, block H, jump Space, dodge Left Shift, skills = - 0
Stamina the green bar; attacks, blocks, dodges and sprinting drain it. Run dry and you are slow and weak until it refills
Tap dodge rolls out of harm's way; hold it while moving to sprint
Backstab hits from behind deal 60% more damage and cannot be blocked: flank, cloak, or teleport behind
Parry raise your block just as a punch or kick lands to stop it dead and stagger the attacker
Power-ups walk through a floating gem: health, shield, rage, haste, mana, cloak or vampire
Formation stay within a few steps of a teammate and you both take 15% less damage
Tournaments friendly fire is on (careful with your area attacks), and a downed teammate can be revived by standing over them
Sudden death a ring of fire closes in and burns anyone outside it
Esc or P pauses the match

Some keyboards cannot register many keys at once. If a move does not fire while several people are holding keys, rebind to keys further apart.
